Lead Quest: how we use your details

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Lead Quest is made by Dr. Arowolo Ayoola. It is free and has no adverts.

What stays on this device

Your child's progress, stars, cards, badges and voice messages are saved only on this device. They leave it only if you save a backup file yourself.

What we receive when you register

Your child's first name or nickname, age, boy or girl, country, a player number made by the app, and simple progress counts (stories finished, stars and badges). We only receive a parent or guardian's email and phone number if you tick the consent box.

Why

To know how many children use Lead Quest and where, to make it better, and, only with your consent, to send you occasional encouragement and news of new books.

Where it is kept

In a private Google Sheet in Dr. Ayoola's Google account. We never sell your details or share them with advertisers.

Children

We ask for a parent's or guardian's email, not the child's, and only with a parent's agreement. Using Lead Quest without registering is always fine.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can remove your details at any time in the Parent corner, under Family registration. You can also reply to any Lead Quest email to ask for a copy of your details or for them to be deleted.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change too.
